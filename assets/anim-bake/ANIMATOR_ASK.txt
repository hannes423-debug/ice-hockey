TO THE ANIMATOR - what we found in the two blends, and the one thing we need
updated 2026-08-17

Short version: your note about the two files solved the grip, the newer pack is
now in the game using your own stick, and there is exactly ONE thing left that
we cannot do from our side. It is at the bottom under "THE ASK".


WHAT YOUR NOTE UNLOCKED

We ignored the FK* actions as you said. The IK blend gave us everything: the
shaft is held rigidly by the LOWER hand, with the top hand acting as a hinge on
the knob. That let us measure the stick as fixed points in the lower hand's
local space, one set per grip:

    neutral     butt [ 0.0104  0.1417 -0.7067]   tip [ 0.1551 -0.0342  0.8709]
    forehand    butt [ 0.0619  0.1410 -0.6117]   tip [ 0.0785 -0.0518  0.9704]
    backhand    butt [ 0.0233  0.1332 -0.6125]   tip [ 0.1552  0.0289  0.9726]
    with puck   butt [-0.0240  0.1198 -0.5393]   tip [ 0.2967  0.0859  1.0217]

Shaft length 1.594 m. Checked by rebuilding each clip's stick from a different
clip's numbers: 0 to 11 mm and 0.03 degrees out.

You were also right that the newer clips start from idle poses that already have
the stick. That is what makes the newer pack work even though it carries no
stick data. Seven of those clips are now in the game taking their shaft straight
from your grip, with no processing from us at all:

    IdleForeHandPulledBack, IdleBackHandToForeHand, IdleForeHandToBackHand,
    SpinoramaL, SpinoramaR, WindmillDekeL, WindmillDekeR

Blade height in the running game matches your blend to within about 3 mm on
every one of them, and the windmill correctly lifts to about 1 m.


TWO THINGS NOT COVERED

1. Neutral has no stick pose in either file. In the Neutral idle the arms are at
   the sides, and a stick held by those hands ends up about 1.04 m in the air.
   The four transitions that touch Neutral are right at their forehand and
   backhand ends and wrong at the neutral end. Not urgent, we can treat Neutral
   as a stickless glide. If Neutral is meant to be a stick-carrying stance, it
   needs re-posing too, and then it drops straight in like the seven above.

2. THE ASK. These six still look wrong in game:

       WalkForward   WalkBackward   Acceleration
       GlideForward  Stop           StopHockey

   They are the earliest batch, from before the stick control existed, and they
   are the ordinary skating states, so they are on screen more than anything
   else in the build.

   Their hands sit at the correct 0.640 m spacing but not at a stick-holding
   orientation, so locking the stick to them puts the blade 0.55 to 1.24 m off
   the ice. We also tried solving a grip for them rather than transplanting one:
   the six answers disagree with each other by up to 112 degrees, which means
   there is no single stick those six hands could all be holding.

   Please note that keying the `stick` control in those six will NOT fix them.
   The control follows the hands, and in those six the hands are not holding
   anything.

   What we need is the arms re-posed, the same way you did the newer pack:
   start each of the six from a stance idle that already holds the stick, then
   animate the skating on top. Delivered that way they need nothing from us,
   exactly like the seven that are already in.


ONE SMALL NOTE, NO ACTION NEEDED

ForeHand is byte identical to IdleL, BackHand to IdleR, and Neutral to IdleN in
the newer file. We checked at the raw curve level, so they are duplicates made
in Blender rather than an export fault. We just use one of each.
